# MLFQ edge cases, hand-computed in docs/checks/mlfq.md:
#   ./ossim workloads/mlfq_check.txt --sched mlfq --quantum 2 --no-paging --gantt
# A alone keeps the CPU after its quantum and sinks to level 2. B (level 0)
# preempts A (level 1) at once. The boost at t=20 puts A back on level 0, so
# C, arriving at t=21, has to wait for the end of A's level-0 quantum.

process A arrive=0 pages=1
    cpu 30
end

process B arrive=3 pages=1
    cpu 2
end

process C arrive=21 pages=1
    cpu 2
end
